# 🐉 Gamified Writing Suite for Obsidian (AuDHD Author Edition)

> **A low-demand, hyperfocus-friendly writing suite designed by and for AuDHD women authors.**

Standard writing advice tells you to "write 1,000 words every single day at 5:00 AM." But if your brain relies on interest-led momentum, cycle-aware energy levels, and hyperfocus bursts, rigid rules just lead to burnout. 

This Obsidian plugin replaces shame-based productivity with a **900-Level Legendary Prestige loop** inspired by *Stardew Valley* farming and *D&D* combat mechanics. It tracks your words, honors your energy reserves, and rewards every single interaction with your manuscript.

---

## ⚡ Built for Your Brain & Your Cycle

* **Cycle & Energy Syncing:** Easily track your daily energy (1–5 stars) and hormonal phase (*Menstruation, Follicular, Ovulation, Luteal*). Honor low-estrogen rest phases just as much as high-estrogen drafting surges.
* **Friction-Free XP & Word Tracking:** Real-time session monitoring that tracks words added, net speed (WPM), character counts, and pause intervals—without punishing you for taking breaks.
* **Dynamic Gamification Tiers:** Earn tier-based rewards as you level up from Level 1 all the way to Level 900!
  * **Crops & Animals Mode (Stardew Style):** Harvest Parsnips, Strawberries, and Sweet Gem Berries, or care for Tier 3 Barn Animals.
  * **Monster Slayer Mode (D&D Style):** Slay Imps, Goblins, Trolls, and Dragons.
* **AuDHD Affirmation Engine:** Built-in encouraging prompts written specifically to validate neurodivergent writing habits, sensory limits, and non-linear momentum.
* **Automated Log Vaulting:** Automatically exports detailed Markdown session reports to your designated vault folder so you can keep a low-effort record of your progress over time.
* **Notes:** There are currently only 3 tiers. Tier 1 is the first 300 levels. Tier 2 are levels 300 - 600. Tier 3 (final) are 600 - 900 levels. 

---

## 🎮 How It Works

1. **Set Your Vibe:** Open the sidebar tab (`Gamepad` icon) and choose your theme mode (**Crops & Animals** or **Monsters**).
2. **Log Energy & Cycle:** Select your current cycle phase and daily energy level before starting.
3. **Start Writing:** Hit **Start Writing Session** while working in any Markdown note. The plugin silently monitors your progress in real time.
4. **Claim Your Rewards:** Finish your session to earn XP, unlock inventory collectibles, and auto-export a comprehensive progress log into your vault.

## 🛠️ Installation

### Option 1: Via BRAT (Recommended for Easy Updates)
1. Install the **Obsidian42 - BRAT** community plugin in Obsidian.
2. Open BRAT settings and click **Add Beta Plugin**.
3. Paste this repository's GitHub URL: `https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME/YOUR_REPO_NAME`
4. Click **Add Plugin** and enable **Gamified Writing Suite** under your installed plugins!

### Option 2: Manual Installation
1. Download the latest release files (`main.js`, `manifest.json`, `styles.css`).
2. Create a folder named `gamified-writing-suite` inside your vault at `.obsidian/plugins/`.
3. Move the downloaded files into `.obsidian/plugins/gamified-writing-suite/`.
4. Reload Obsidian (`Ctrl + R` / `Cmd + R`) and enable the plugin in **Settings > Community plugins**.
